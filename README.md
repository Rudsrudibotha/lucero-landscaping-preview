# Lucero Landscaping & Hardscaping: website

Single-page site for Lucero Landscaping LLC (Lucero Landscaping & Hardscaping), serving Lewes, Rehoboth Beach, Milton, Dewey Beach, Georgetown, Long Neck and surrounding areas in Delaware: lawn care, landscaping, hardscaping, snow removal, and tree removal, junk removal and storm cleanup.

## Structure
- `index.html`: hero with the gravel side-yard before-and-after photo, towns served, tree and junk removal section, services, photos of our work, Nextdoor Fave award and our story, free-estimate form, mobile call/text bar
- `styles.css`: stylesheet (greens and sun gold from the logo)
- `script.js`: mobile menu (Escape closes) and the estimate form, which builds an email (or a text) with the details filled in and asks for photos of the job
- `images/`: optimised JPGs and icons
- `SOURCES.txt`: image credits
